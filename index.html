About Bluepeak

We started as three friends who wanted to make websites that look calm and work well. Today we are a team of designers, developers and marketers helping clients in many countries.

Every project follows the same rule: understand the goal first, then design, build, test and launch. You always know what we are doing and why.

How we work

  1. Discover. We learn about your goals, audience and budget.
  2. Design. We draw layouts and you approve them before coding.
  3. Build. We write the code and test it on desktop, tablet and mobile.
  4. Launch. We publish your project and stay with you after launch.

Why choose us

  • Clear prices with no hidden costs
  • Weekly progress updates
  • Mobile friendly on every project
  • 30 days of free support after launch
